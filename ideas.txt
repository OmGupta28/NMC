okay so i got some numbers too
I require 4 cycles to collect 3 IMOs from the same SRAM Bank(serially collecting each IMO for the respective compute cores)
6 cycles to finish their computation
1 cycle to again start collecting the IMOs and new BO for the next computation
Therefore, when BO is 5 bits wide, the result collection will take 6 cycles,
for N bit BO, N + 1 cycles are required
same logic for IMO collection\
the re-computation cycle will be constant at 1 cycle

therefore, for this configuration of BO = 5 bits, 3 IMOs fetched from the same SRAM bank costs be 11 cycles in total.
